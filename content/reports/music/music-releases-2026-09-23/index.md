## 1. Brat — *Manslayer* (out Sept 18; new single "Speculum Bleed" Sept 22)

The week's most on-lane **actual album release**. Glasgow-forged heavy unit **Brat**
— who sit somewhere between grunge, hardcore, and noisy metal, in the
Full-of-Hell / Chat-Pile-adjacent orbit — dropped *Manslayer* on Prosthetic Records
last Friday (Sept 18). Reviews landed across the weekend (therazorsedge Sept 20,
gbhbl Sept 22), and on Tuesday (Sept 22) they delivered a follow-up single, the
snarling **"Speculum Bleed"** (via Revolver/Chaoszine), keeping the campaign hot.
If your noise-rock lane has room for a band that marries filthy riff-work with
genuine hardcore snarl, this is the one to spin this week. *Status:* Album out;
new single out Sept 22. *Sources:* gbhbl, therazorsedge.rocks, Revolver, Chaoszine
(Sept 2026).

## 2. Soul Glo — *NA I'M GON GRAB ALLAT* (guest list grows — Sept 23)

The Philadelphia noise-hardcore trio's follow-up to *Diaspora Problems* remains the

## 3. The Number Twelve Looks Like You — *Howls From The Holographic Heart* (announced; out Oct 16)

Mathcore/experimental veterans **The Number Twelve Looks Like You** announced a new
album, *Howls From The Holographic Heart*, out **October 16** via Equal Vision
Records (announced Sept 17, picked up by Punknews Sept 23). They unveiled lead track
**"Glow In The Dark Skin"** with a video by Jesse Korman (and animation by Jimmy
Lazer), and will tour the US for the **20th anniversary of *Mongrel*** from late
September into October. For the mathcore end of your Converge/Go It Alone interest,
this is a strong October marker. *Status:* Announced — out Oct 16, US tour Sept
25–Oct 30. *Sources:* Punknews, BroadwayWorld, Lambgoat (Sept 2026).

## 4. Deady — *HUNKA JUNK* (digital out Sept 22; physical Sept 25)

The Louisville punk band **Deady** released *HUNKA JUNK* digitally **today**
(Sept 22), with the vinyl following **Sept 25** via Romanus Records. The album packs
twelve tracks; Punknews ran a full track-by-track breakdown with lyricist/vocalist
Mandy Keathley (single "Systematic Slaughter" opens the record). It's a scrappy,
sharp-edged punk record rather than a genre-lane bomb, but it's a genuine new LP
dropping in your window and worth a spin for the harder-punk side of the list.
*Status:* Out now digitally; physical Sept 25. *Source:* Punknews (Sept 2026).
