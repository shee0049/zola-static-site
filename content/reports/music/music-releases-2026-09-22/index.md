## 1. Protomartyr — *Hotel Usona* (out Sept 18)

The most on-target release this week for your post-punk lane. Protomartyr —
Detroit's literate, bass-forward art-punks fronted by Joe Casey — dropped
*Hotel Usona* on Sept 18, their first record since 2025's *Maggots in the
Silver Chair* era. Early reviews (Northern Transmissions, No More Workhorse)
frame it as a return to the taut, noirish post-punk they've built their
reputation on, with the band rejecting a fine-arts purism in favor of
something that actually stomps. They capped the week with a second single,
"Exalted Eagles" (Sept 21). If you liked *Relatives in Descent* or *Ultimate
Ultimate Success Today*, this is the week's must-listen. *Status:* Out now.
*Sources:* Northern Transmissions, No More Workhorse, mxdwn (Sept 18–22 2026).

## 2. Chat Pile — *Who Loves the Sun* + "Shrine" video & EU tour (news Sept 15–18)

Chat Pile remain the #1 act on your noise-rock/sludge watchlist, and their
third album *Who Loves the Sun* (out Sept 4 on The Flenser) is the anchor.
This week they kept the campaign moving: a video for album track "Shrine"
(Sept 18, via New Noise) and an announced European tour (Sept 15, via
Punknews). The record has drawn a torrent of reviews — Pitchfork, The
Guardian, Exclaim!, Paste, The Needle Drop (Sept 4–21) — with most praising
the slow-motion-apocalypse dread and frontman Ray Gun Bush's howl, while a
few critics noted the band leaning into a more alt-rock rebrand. Either way,
it is squarely your lane: sludge, noise, and dread in equal measure.
*Status:* Out now. *Sources:* Pitchfork, The Guardian, Exclaim!, New Noise
Magazine, Punknews (Sept 2026).

## 3. Knocked Loose — *Laugh Tracks* 10th-anniversary reissue (Sept 17)

The hardcore watchlist's flagship modern band turned back the clock: Knocked
Loose announced a 10th-anniversary vinyl reissue of *Laugh Tracks* — their
2016 breakthrough that flipped the genre into its aggressive, down-tuned
modern sound — alongside a merch capsule (Sept 17, ThePRP). It's a reissue
rather than new music, but it ties into their still-active cycle with Denzel
Curry (the "Hive Mind" collab and the fall 2026 co-headlining tour). For the
Knocked Loose / Go It Alone / Speed corner of your list, this is the
nostalgia beat of the week. *Status:* Reissue announced Sept 17. *Source:*
ThePRP (Sept 2026).
