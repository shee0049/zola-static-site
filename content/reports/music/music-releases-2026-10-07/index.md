## 1. Haywire — *I Don't Wanna Say Goodbye* (out Oct 2, Roadrunner Records)

The most on-lane release of the day and the clear hardcore pick. **Boston hardcore** unit **Haywire** released their **Roadrunner Records full-length debut**, ***I Don't Wanna Say Goodbye***, **Friday, Oct 2** — and it's now in the middle of a proper critic sweep: **Kerrang!** (Sep 29) and **New Noise** (Oct 2) reviewed it around release, **Rolling Stone** ran a feature ("Haywire Are Living Their Hardcore Dreams," Oct 6) profiling the band's momentum, and **Pitchfork** published its review **today, Oct 7**. Preceded by the explosive **"Interstate Love Affair"** and **"Sincerely Yours,"** the record follows up their 2024 DAZE debut *Conditioned for Demolition* with the modern, pummeling New England hardcore that's been tearing up Sound & Fury-style bills. *Status:* **Out Oct 2** (citing MB release date; Pitchfork/RS/Kerrang reviews). *Sources:* Pitchfork (Oct 7), Rolling Stone (Oct 6), Kerrang! (Sep 29), New Noise (Oct 2), MusicBrainz (2026-10-02).

## 2. BOSS KELOID — *Float & Aperture* (out Oct 23, Church Road Records)

For the **sludge / stoner / proggy** corner of the wheel. **BOSS KELOID**, the UK outfit known for their progressive-rock-meets-sludge-and-stoner-metal blend, confirmed ***Float & Aperture*** for **October 23 via Church Road Records** — their first since 2021's *Family The Smiling Thrush*. On **Oct 6** they shared **"Pedestal,"** the second single and one of the album's standouts (a tracklist that also runs "Breeze," "Landslide," "Weight," "Aros," "Accord" and "Sanguine"), following the lead single **"Landslide."** The record was written through a more collaborative band-wide songwriting process, with vocalist Alex Hurst framing it around embracing life and rolling with the punches. For the Everything-adjacent sludge/psych crowd this is a dependable, slightly-off-mainstream autumn pickup. *Status:* **Oct 23** pre-order now; "Pedestal" out Oct 6, "Landslide" out now. *Sources:* Distorted Sound (Sep 3), Lambgoat (Oct 6).

## 3. Crisis Actor — *Limited Hangout* (out Nov 27, Alternative Tentacles)

A strong **post-punk / hardcore** announcement, premiered this week by Punknews. **Crisis Actor**, the L.A. crew who've built recent heat on a mix of **early-LA-punk destruction and early-'80s goth coldness**, have a new record out **November 27 via Alternative Tentacles** (the Dead Kennedys-founded label). ***Limited Hangout*** finds them doubling down on that menacing-and-fierce sound while holding viewpoints that are at once hopeful and nihilistic; the leaked track **"The Big Fall"** — which bassist/video director Zach Crawford frames as "bearing witness to the deterioration of empire" — premiered with a video. If the earlier "Slave New World" era is your entry point, this is squarely in the art-punk/punk-goth zone between your hardcore and post-punk lanes. *Status:* **Nov 27**; "The Big Fall" out now. *Sources:* Punknews.org (Oct 6).

## 4. BALACLAVA — *Money Glitch* (out Jan 22, 2027, Mothland)

The debut-album announcement for the **punk** lane. **Queens, New York-based punk rockers BALACLAVA** announced they'll release their first full-length, ***Money Glitch***, on **January 22 via Mothland**, and put out a video for the new song **"WHAT A REGULAR DAY"** (directed by the band with Anthony Earabino and Matt Elicone). It follows their 2024 EP *The 1/4 Inch Almanac*. Farther out than the rest of this week's slate, but a legitimately on-lane label sign to keep on your radar for the new year. *Status:* **Jan 22, 2027**; "WHAT A REGULAR DAY" out now. *Sources:* Punknews.org (Oct 6).
