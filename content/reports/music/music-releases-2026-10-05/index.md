## 1. Assumption — *Paradox Of The Arrow* (out Nov 13, 20 Buck Spin)

The weekend's most on-lane announcement, and a genuine **death-doom** pick for the sludge corner. Italian outfit **Assumption** — who have quietly built a following with crushing, atmospheric doom — announced their **third studio album**, ***Paradox Of The Arrow***, due **Friday, November 13 via 20 Buck Spin**. It follows 2022's *Hadean Tides* and pushes further into the band's blend of gut-punching death-doom, spacey atmospheric passages and psychedelic flourishes. Named after Zeno's *paradox of motion*, the record explores movement, inertia and time, and was mixed/mastered by **Claudio Mulas** with artwork by **Mariya Popyk**. The first single, **"Tombs Upon The Sun,"** is streaming now — an expansive, mostly-accessible cut about "an imaginary, reverse oceanic world to which our spirit is set to return." At 20 Buck Spin it sits in excellent slow-and-heavy company. *Status:* Out **Nov 13**; "Tombs Upon The Sun" out now, pre-orders live. *Sources:* Distorted Sound (Oct 4).

## 2. Graveyard — *Fever* (out Oct 9, Nuclear Blast)

A big **stoner / retro-heavy rock** flag for this Friday. Gothenburg's **Graveyard** — legends of the late-2000s bluesy-heaviness revival — release ***Fever*** on **Friday, October 9 via Nuclear Blast**, their first full-length in several years and a hotly-anticipated comeback for the band. Two singles have already landed: **"Tongue Tied"** (with video, mid-September) and the doomier **"A Means To An End"** (August), both carrying the band's signature warm, fuzz-kissed riffing and road-weary vocals. If *Hisingen Blues*-era Graveyard lives on your shelf, *Fever* is the most straightforward pickup of the coming week, sitting neatly alongside the Sabbath/Clutch-adjacent end of your catalogue. *Status:* Out **Oct 9**. *Sources:* The Razor's Edge release round-up (Oct 9), Chaoszine (Sept 18), Blabbermouth (Aug 14), Distorted Sound (Aug 15).

## 3. Smoulder — *Witch Wife In An Alien World* (out Oct 9, Cruz Del Sur)

Epic doom for the coming Friday, from a Canadian band you should know. Toronto trio **Smoulder** release their eagerly-awaited follow-up ***Witch Wife In An Alien World*** on **October 9 via Cruz Del Sur Music**, and the label full-length keeps the band's signature galloping, trad-metal-inflected **epic doom** — soaring chest-belted vocals over riffs indebted to Candlemass and Cirith Ungol. The Razor's Edge singles it out as "one of today's most underrated acts." With its sword-and-sorcery atmosphere and clean production, it's a natural for the king-woman / Ragana-adjacent, melody-forward end of your doom taste. *Status:* Out **Oct 9**. *Sources:* The Razor's Edge release round-up (Oct 9), iTunes (2026-10-09).

## 4. Sam and Dean — *Good-Time End-Times Apocalypse BBQ* (out Oct 23, Powertone)

A fresh indie/punk pairing with real pedigree. Dead Milkmen alumni **Samuel Locke Ward** and drummer **Dean Clean** have joined forces as **Sam and Dean**, and their debut together, ***Good-Time End-Times Apocalypse BBQ***, is out **October 23 via Powertone Records**, with the lead single streaming now. Ward has long mixed punk, outsider humor and unvarnished folk-rock, and pairing him with Dean's steady backbeat makes for the kind of scrappy, literate, deadpan indie you'd expect from the Dead Milkmen orbit — squarely in the loose indie/punk lane of your list. *Status:* Out **Oct 23**; lead single out now. *Sources:* Punknews.org (Oct 4).

## 5. MC Lars — *The Edgar Allan Poe LP* (expanded, out Oct 16, Oglio)

A nerdcore alt-rap reissue for the left field. **MC Lars** has dropped **"Amontillado,"** a new track based on the Edgar Allan Poe story of the same name, as the lead-out from the **new/expanded version of his *The Edgar Allan Poe LP***, arriving **October 16 via Oglio Entertainment**. The original Poe LP was one of the first hip-hop records built entirely around the gothic author, and this revisiting threads the 2026 relaunch together — a curiosity for the rap/humor intersection on your watchlist rather than a heavy-hitter. *Status:* LP out **Oct 16**; "Amontillado" streaming now. *Sources:* Punknews.org (Oct 4).
