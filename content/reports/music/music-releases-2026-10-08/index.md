## 1. Graveyard — *Fever* (out Friday, Oct 9, Nuclear Blast)

The easy pick for the **sludge / retro-doom** end of the wheel. Sweden's **Graveyard** return with ***Fever*** — their first full-length since 2023's *6* — out **Friday, Oct 9 via Nuclear Blast**. The band remains in its classic fuzz-heavy, psych-blues "graveyard boogie" form — thick riffs, warm organ, blues-rock swagger. If your taste sits next to earlier records like *Hisingen Blues* and *Lights Out*, this is the most reliable full-length of the week for your collection, and a natural companion to the heavier sludge/doom rotation you already keep. *Status:* **Out Oct 9.** *Sources:* Grande Rock webzine, The Razor's Edge (Oct 9 round-up), Ghost Cult Magazine (Oct 9 metal release radar).

## 2. Smoulder — *Witch Wife In An Alien World* (out Friday, Oct 9, Cruz Del Sur)

Staying in the doom lane but going **epic/traditional** rather than bluesy. Toronto's **Smoulder** — a band squarely in the Candlemass-to-Satan tradition — release their sophomore full-length ***Witch Wife In An Alien World*** on **Oct 9 via Cruz Del Sur Music**. As previewed in the Oct 5 edition, it doubles down on their twin-guitar, galloping, fantasy-lyric heavy metal, produced with a live-room warmth that suits the low-and-slow end of your taste. For listeners who keep Smoulder next to King Woman and Ragana on the Sabbath-derived axis, this is the weekend's most on-lane Canadian release. *Status:* **Out Oct 9**; pre-order live at Bandcamp. *Sources:* Cruz Del Sur (Bandcamp album page), The Razor's Edge / Ghost Cult Oct 9 radars.

## 4. Darkest Hour — "Warrior" single + *Suffer Divine* (album Oct 30)

**Announced today (Oct 8):** DC metalcore/melodic-death vets **Darkest Hour** shared a new single and video, **"Warrior,"** and confirmed a **December 2026 headlining U.S. tour**. It's the next preview from their upcoming album ***Suffer Divine***, out **Oct 30** (the band's first full-length in about three years). Darkest Hour sit at the harder, mid-paced edge of your hardcore/post-hardcore lane rather than the noisier end — the "Warrior" push riff is pure late-career shoulder-charge metal — and the new-year tour gives you a live reason to check in. *Status:* Single out now (Oct 8), *Suffer Divine* out **Oct 30**. *Sources:* Metalheads Forever Magazine (Oct 8), Metalheads Forever (album announcement, Sep 2).

## 5. 72 Rats — *it all burns down someday* (out Oct 9, streaming)

The edge-punk entry on tomorrow's pile. **72 Rats** — the Ross Robinson-produced punk/hardcore crew that re-surfaced this year — put ***it all burns down someday*** on streaming **Oct 9** (physicals have been trickling out). It's chaotic, shouting, borderline-industrial hardcore that sits comfortably in the same lane as Drug Church, Show Me the Body and the noisier sections of your collection. *Status:* **Streaming Oct 9**; physicals out now. *Sources:* Bandcamp album page, The Razor's Edge / Ghost Cult Oct 9 radars.
