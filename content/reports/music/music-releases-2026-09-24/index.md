## 1. Gilla Band — *Pugnello* (out Sept 25, Rough Trade)

The biggest on-lane arrival of the week lands **tomorrow (Friday, Sept 25)** via
Rough Trade. **Gilla Band** — the Dublin quartet that couples post-punk tension
to genuinely brutal noise-rock dynamics — return with *Pugnello*, their first
album since 2022's *Most Normal*. It's a four-year build: singer **Dara Kiely**
took up painting, guitarist **Alan Duggan Borges** launched solo project Null
Club, bassist/producer **Daniel Fox** produced for Sprints, Lambrini Girls and
Mandy, Indiana, and drummer Adam Faulkner toured with Lankum. The record was
cut 2022–2026 across three Dublin studios and is previewed by the singles
**"Giraffe"** (May) and the superb **"Placeholder"**, which Kiely frames around
mental-health comfort distractions and childhood nostalgia ("I said I wanted to be a Power Ranger… I've never forgiven them"). If *Most Normal* was in your rotation, this is the week's must-check release. *Status:* Out tomorrow, Sept 25. *Sources:* Rough Trade, The Line of Best Fit, Stereogum, BrooklynVegan (Sept 2026).

## 2. Thunder Queens — *Deep Below* (out today, Sept 24, Victory Pool)

A real album drops **today** on Victory Pool Records from Montreal hardcore unit

Canadian side of the list, this is a genuine on-tour new LP to spin. *Status:*
Out today, Sept 24. *Sources:* Punknews.org, Bandcamp (Victory Pool), Sept 2026.

## 4. Bad Brains — *Are You Ready – The First Studio Recording* (announced; out Nov 6)

One for the classic-hardcore firebrand flank: **Bad Brains** will release *Are
You Ready – The First Studio Recording*, an archival session taped at DC's
**Inner Ear** studio in **March 1979** — the earliest known studio recordings of
the band, cut in an hour as a six-song session. The record has been **newly
remixed by Ian MacKaye** and arrives via **Trust Records on November 6**
(tracks: "Pay to Cum," "Don't Bother Me," "Black Dots," "Migraine," "Send No
Flowers," "Jamming at the Atlantis"). On Nov 5, bassist **Darryl Jenifer** and
MacKaye discuss the session at the MLK Jr. Memorial Library, with a Bad Brains
archival exhibition opening the next day. Essential provenance for the ancestral
end of your hardcore collection. *Status:* Announced — out Nov 6. *Source:*
Punknews.org (Sept 2026).

## 5. Misfits — *American Psycho* (reissue announced; out Nov 13)

The **Misfits** are reissuing their first **Michale Graves**-era record,
1997's *American Psycho* — the one Glenn Danzig famously doesn't sing on. The
new edition is **newly remastered** and comes via **Interscope on November 13**
with two bonus tracks, **"Dead Kings Rise"** and **"Hell Night."** A clean
touchstone for the heritage/horror-punk flank of the list, and a nice pairing
with the Bad Brains archive above. *Status:* Announced — out Nov 13. *Source:*
Punknews.org (Sept 2026).

## 6. Live Skull — *What Could Go Wrong?* (announced; out Nov 13)

The New York noise-rock veterans **Live Skull** — formed 1982, key figures in
the city's abrasive early-'80s underground alongside Sonic Youth — return to
their improvisational roots on *What Could Go Wrong?*, out **November 13** via
**Bronson Recordings**. It's their fourth studio album since reuniting, a
nine-track record assembled from free jams pulled apart into songs (the
current lineup of Mark C., Rich Hutchins, Dave Hollinghurst and Colyn Hunt).
The single **"Internal Combustion"** (Sept 10) previews the combustive tone;
titles like "Reality Is Optional" and "4th Conspiracy" reflect a renewed focus
on American politics and authoritarian drift. Recorded at Mark C.'s Deepsea
studio in Hoboken — the building also housed Yo La Tengo and Sonic Youth. Pure
noise-rock pedigree for your lane. *Status:* Announced — out Nov 13. *Source:*
That Eric Alper (Sept 22 2026).
