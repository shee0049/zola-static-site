1. Comeback Kid — Chosen Hell (+ new single "Yesterday's Gone" feat. Brendan Garrone)

The most on-watchlist item of the day. Winnipeg's Comeback Kid — a cornerstone of the modern Canadian hardcore scene and a clean fit for the Knocked Loose / Drug Church end of your list — have their eighth album, Chosen Hell, locked for November 6 via SharpTone Records (U.S.) / Dine Alone Records (Canada). They'd already previewed it with the title track and "Shedding Season" (feat. Spiritbox's Courtney LaPlante); on Oct 1 they followed with "Yesterday's Gone," featuring Incendiary's Brendan Garrone — a straight hardcore collaboration that lands right in your wheelhouse. The album also arrives with a fall U.S. tour alongside Defeater, Dead Heat and The Warriors. Status: Album out Nov 6; single "Yesterday's Gone" out Oct 1. Sources: Chaoszine (Oct 1 2026), Bandcamp, Heaven's Metal Magazine / press release (Sept 24 2026).

2. Tear Dungeon — Squirm (out Oct 2, TODO Records) + North American tour

A fresh hardcore-punk arrival for Oct 2. Austin, TX masked menaces Tear Dungeon release their new album Squirm on TODO Records this Friday, and this week announced a sprawling fall North American tour supporting it — kicking off with an Oct 9 hometown show and running through Nov 22 in Houston, with Canadian stops in Toronto (Nov 4, Dance Cave) and Montreal (Nov 5, Toscadura). The track "Freak Flag" previews it. Reviews this week describe the record as a furious, feedback-drenched punk assault — squarely in the hardcore/post-hardcore lane of the playlist. Status: Out Fri, Oct 2; tour through Nov 22. Sources: Exclaim! (Sept 30 2026), Glide Magazine (Oct 1 2026), V13 Media / Punknews (Oct 1 2026).

3. Fake Names — Punk Ruins (out Oct 23, Epitaph) + "If You Could See Me Today"

For the post-punk / classic-adjacent stretch of your list. Fake Names — the punk supergroup of vocalist Dennis Lyxzén (Refused, INVSN), guitarist Brian Baker (Bad Religion, Minor Threat, Dag Nasty) and drummer Mario Rubalcaba (Rocket from the Crypt, OFF!) — share their third full-length, Punk Ruins, on October 23 via Epitaph. On Sept 29–30 they unveiled the latest single, "If You Could See Me Today," a jangle-pop turn (recorded at Chicago's Electrical Audio with engineer Matthew Barnhardy) that keeps the Refused-lineage thread running right into your wheelhouse. Status: Album out Oct 23; "If You Could See Me Today" out Sept 29–30. Sources: Exclaim! (Sept 2 2026), Consequence / Stereoboard / Blabbermouth (Sept 29 2026).

4. Niis — "No Flowers" video + EP Play Niis (out Oct 23, Rise Records)

A fresh punk watch. Niis, who released the acclaimed Niis World in 2025, dropped a video for the new song "No Flowers" (directed by Marie Ramos) on Sept 30, and confirmed it's from their upcoming EP Play Niis — out October 23 via Rise Records. They kick off a U.S. tour in October (showbox in Seattle Oct 7). It's a tight, noisy punk/post-hardcore pickup for the growing-labels side of the watchlist. Status: EP out Oct 23; "No Flowers" video out Sept 30. Sources: Punknews.org (Sept 30 2026).

5. Ov Ruin — The Hollow Reign (EP complete with final title track)

The heavier, closer-to-metal end of today's slate. Deathcore act Ov Ruin completed their The Hollow Reign EP with the release of the final title track (featuring Cole Odom) on Oct 1 — following the singles "Crowned in Absence" and "A Throne of Nothing." It leans into the modern deathcore/hardcore crossover space; a useful spot-check for the heavier edge of the playlist rather than a core-lane headline. Status: EP complete Oct 1. Sources: Chaoszine (Oct 1 2026).
