Executive Summary

A genuinely busy week across the noise-rock, sludge, hardcore and post-punk lanes — most of it announced in the last 48 hours, so almost everything below is new to your radar. Headliners: Cult Crime, the Toronto punk four-piece of Psychic Void's Matthew Menard, will drop their sophomore attack Fully Automatic on Oct 30 via Static Shock (trashy Ramones/Dead Boys-style punk — clean fit for your punk lane). Seattle sludge-meets-hardcore bruisers Great Falls (ex-members of Kiss It Goodbye and Undertow) return with The Bite That Grows Up on Oct 16 via Neurot Recordings. In a big one for the ancestry lane, Bad Brains announced Are You Ready: The First Studio Recording (Inner Ear – March 1979) — six of the band's earliest recordings, mixed by Ian MacKaye, out Nov 6 via Trust Records with the unreleased first take of "Pay to Cum." Also landing: Swedish d-beat/crust institution Wolfbrigade with Howl from Underground (Oct 9, Metal Blade), UK post-hardcore/post-punk trio Bat Flattery with a self-titled debut (Oct 9), Toronto punk band Bad Waitress with In The Hollow (Oct 30, Stomp Records), and T.S.O.L. frontman Jack Grisham's new project Jack Grisham & the Life Undone (Oct 30, Lost in Berlin). Nothing below repeats earlier reports — all of it surfaced since Thursday's edition.

1. Cult Crime — Fully Automatic (out Oct 30, Static Shock)

The most on-lane punk item of the day. What started as a solo project for Psychic Void's Matthew Menard has grown into a four-piece Toronto band whose ranks draw from Chain Whip, Imploders, and the Vapids — pedigree that shows in the sound. After a well-received self-titled debut last year, they're already back with the sophomore LP Fully Automatic, out October 30 via Static Shock Records. Two tracks are streaming now — "Final Dose" and "Just Like a Knife" — and Stereogum describes the pair as grimy, reckless rock 'n' roll with a lot of Ramones and Dead Boys in the DNA: basement punk, snotty and trashy. It's a straight hit for the punk/post-hardcore end of your playlist. Status: Out Oct 30; two tracks streaming now. Sources: Stereogum (Oct 1), Bandcamp.

2. Great Falls — The Bite That Grows Up (out Oct 16, Neurot Recordings)

A core-lane arrival for the noise-rock/sludge stretch of the list. Great Falls — the Seattle trio of guitarist/vocalist Demian Johnston, bassist Shane Mehling and drummer/vocalist Nickolis Parks, formed from members of Kiss It Goodbye, Undertow, Playing Enemy, Bastard Feast and Gaytheist — release their roughly forty-minute The Bite That Grows Up on October 16 via Neurot Recordings. Reviews frame it as sludge-meets-hardcore fury with post-metal melancholy, dissonant riffs that turn on a dime, and an all-encompassing low-end. This week they shared the second single, "Debt Ethics," which Johnston says was sparked by his child running away from home — the event that inspired the whole record. The earlier single "Nicotine in the Spine" features vocals by the late Peter Clement Lund of the band Kollapse, tracked shortly before his death. Engineered by Scott Evans (Neurosis, Sumac) at Studio Litho, mastered by Brad Boatright at Audiosiege. Status: Out Oct 16 (LP, CD, digital; four vinyl variants). Sources: Rock'n'Load (Sept 27), Distorted Sound (Sept 4), Ghost Cult (Sept 28).

3. Bad Brains — Are You Ready: The First Studio Recording (Inner Ear – March 1979) (out Nov 6, Trust Records)

A landmark for the ancestry / classic-acts corner. Bad Brains will release Are You Ready: The First Studio Recording (Inner Ear – March 1979) on November 6 via Trust Records — six of the band's earliest recordings, captured in a one-hour session at Inner Ear Studios in March 1979 and remixed by Fugazi/Minor Threat legend Ian MacKaye. The lead single is the previously unreleased earliest studio version of "Pay to Cum," with bassist Darryl Jenifer noting the riff drew on the Ramones, The Damned and Return to Forever ("sheer, straight-ahead three-chord mayhem"). The set is being celebrated with a Jenifer/MacKaye conversation at the MLK Jr. Memorial Library on Nov 5 and a Lost Origins Gallery exhibition opening Nov 6. Essential for the hardcore roots of your taste. Status: Out Nov 6; "Pay to Cum" streaming now, pre-orders live. Sources: Consequence (Sept 23), Variety (Sept 23), Pitchfork (Sept 23), Revolver.

4. Wolfbrigade — Howl from Underground (out Oct 9, Metal Blade)

A straight hardcore/d-beat nailing. Swedish crust-punk veterans Wolfbrigade — a mainstay of the Stockholm hardcore scene — release their new full-length Howl from Underground on Friday, October 9 via Metal Blade Records, having already aired the pummeling singles "Cold War Bootlicker", "Doomrunner" and "Anti-Tank Dogs"-adjacent material ahead of it. Working with producer Lawrence Mackrory this time out, the band have said the process shook up their usual approach, leaning into faster, rawer d-beat aggression. It's squarely in the hardcore lane of the watchlist. Status: Out Oct 9 (Metal Blade). Sources: Metal Blade Records (Sept 8), Chaoszine (Sept 8), Lambgoat (Sept 25), Wildfire Music + News (Sept 28).

5. Bat Flattery — Bat Flattery (self-titled, out Oct 9)

A fresh post-hardcore/post-punk pickup. Bat Flattery — a trio from the UK Midlands whose debut album gets a glowing review from The Razor's Edge ("a well-crafted, emotional album that delivers both sonically and cerebrally") — release their self-titled full-length on October 9. The band's brand of melodic, uneasy post-hardcore sits nicely alongside the DEADLETTER / post-punk end of your list. An interview with Rock DNA Magazine (Oct 1) confirms the record is a debut, out this month. Status: Out Oct 9. Sources: The Razor's Edge (Sept 24 review / Oct 1 round-up), Rock DNA Magazine (Oct 1).

6. Bad Waitress — In The Hollow (out Oct 30, Stomp Records)

Canadian punk with a grungy drawl — a natural addition. Bad Waitress, the Toronto band who put out No Taste in 2021, have announced their new album In The Hollow, out October 30 via Stomp Records. This week they shared a video for the new track "I Only Like You When I'm Drunk" (filmed and edited by Danny Alexander). They'll celebrate the record with an album release show Nov 6 at Toronto's Lee's Palace and a Western Canada tour later in November. Status: Out Oct 30; release show Nov 6 at Lee's Palace. Sources: Punknews.org (Oct 2).

7. Jack Grisham & the Life Undone — self-titled (out Oct 30, Lost in Berlin Records)

Another ancestry-lane pick. Jack Grisham — legendary frontman of T.S.O.L. — has teamed with multi-instrumentalist Lars Triesch as Jack Grisham & the Life Undone, and their self-titled debut is out October 30 via Lost in Berlin Records. The new track "Angel Funeral" previews it. It keeps the classic California hardcore (German-tinged) thread running through your classics column. Status: Album out Oct 30; "Angel Funeral" streaming now. Sources: Punknews.org (Oct 2), Apple Music.
