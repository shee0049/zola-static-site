A Sunday edition that catches a fresh burst of announcements made over the last ~48 hours plus a few of this week's genuinely under-covered releases. The headline pick for the indie lane is Wishy's new full-length Nature's Pill, which landed Friday, Oct 2 and led Exclaim!'s new-albums roundup that day. Big news for the sludge/doom lane: a brand-new doom supergroup called Autumn Tomb — uniting Zombi's Steve Moore with The Sword's Bryan Richie and Santiago "Jimmy" Vela III — will release their self-titled debut on Nov 6 via Spaceflight Records, with lead track "Crumbling Thrones" already streaming. 1. Wishy — Nature's Pill (out Oct 2)

The freshest full-length for the indie/acoustic end of your list. Indianapolis trio Wishy — who broke through with the acclaimed 2024 LP Triple Seven and sit neatly in the dreamy-but-punchy shoegaze / power-pop lane — released their new album Nature's Pill on Friday, October 2, 2026. MusicBrainz pins the street date at 2026-10-02, and it appeared at the top of Exclaim!'s "New Album Releases for October 2, 2026" roundup alongside Victoria Monét and Greg Freeman. It follows Paradise on Planet Popstar (2025) and keeps the band's signature blend of chiming, feedback-kissed guitars and hook-forward indie songcraft. If Triple Seven hooked you, this is the week's clear indie-lane must. Status: Out Oct 2. Sources: Exclaim! (Oct 2), MusicBrainz.

2. Autumn Tomb — Autumn Tomb (out Nov 6, Spaceflight Records)

A straight hit for the sludge / doom stretch. Autumn Tomb is a new doom trio teaming Steve Moore (keyboardist of the synth/space-rock duo Zombi, and The Hobbit documentary composer) with The Sword's bassist/keyboardist Bryan Richie and drummer Santiago "Jimmy" Vela III. Moore kicked the project off in 2024 and tracked the debut at his Nightmare City studio in Albany, NY; the session was sparked by Zombi's 2022 tours supporting The Sword and Om. The self-titled album lands November 6 via Spaceflight Records, with the lead track "Crumbling Thrones" streaming now and pre-orders live on Bandcamp. A supergroup, sure — but deliberately so, aiming at a heavy, riff-forward doom record rather than a side-project lark. Essential for the The-Sword-adjacent end of your collection. Status: Out Nov 6. Sources: Theprp (Oct 2), Bandcamp.

3. Gnod — Chronicles of Gnowt (Vol 2) (out Oct 9, Rocket Recordings)

One for the noise / psych / doom file. Salford/Manchester collective Gnod — long-running purveyors of sprawling, trance-like psychedelic noise that veers into doomy drone — return with Chronicles of Gnowt (Vol 2), due Friday, October 9 via Rocket Recordings. It's the second installment of a series that began with Vol 1 back in April 2026 (also on Rocket), and The Razor's Edge lines it up under that week's Doom / Stoner / Psych umbrella. If you like your heaviness viscous and hypnotic rather than riff-shredding, this is a tidy next-week pickup for the noise-rock wing of the playlist. Status: Out Oct 9. Sources: The Razor's Edge (Release Round Up, Oct 9), MusicBrainz.

4. Knives — Nothing New Under The Sun (out Mar 5 2027, Marshall Records)

A fresh post-punk flag for the year-ahead horizon. Bristol collective Knives — who released the well-regarded 2025 debut GLITTER — announced their second album, Nothing New Under The Sun, out March 5, 2027 via Marshall Records, and dropped the first single "Vermillion" (with video) to go with it. The 11-track set (featuring titles like "Helm of the Host," "Janus Sold Me a Broken Promise," "Lip Gossip" and the title track) finds the band writing about consequence and living in line with your values — "every decision you make affects not only yourself but the people around you." Depth-first booker's note: this is squarely a watch-item for the post-punk lane (and the sibling-companion to GLITTER), albeit a good way out. Status: Announced; out Mar 5 2027; "Vermillion" streaming now, pre-orders live. Artwork: TBA (not yet in MusicBrainz). Sources: Distorted Sound (Oct 3), thefestivals.uk (Oct 2).

5. Motörhead — Orgasmatron (deluxe reissue, out November via BMG)

A classics / ancestry pick. Motörhead are re-releasing one of their heaviest records, 1986's Orgasmatron, as a deluxe 3xLP / 2xCD set via BMG in November. The edition includes a brand-new remix of the album by producer Cameron Webb, plus the original album and a raft of outtakes and BBC sessions. The "Orgasmatron" title track — Lemmy's grinding, mid-tempo anthem (and the source of one of his most-quoted monologues) — remains a landmark of heavy, downtuned rock, so a freshly mixed edition is a welcome recontextualisation for the Sabbath-adjacent end of your classics corner. Status: Reissue out November 2026 (BMG). Artwork shown is the original 1986 sleeve. Sources: Punknews.org (Oct 2).
