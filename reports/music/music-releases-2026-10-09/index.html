A proper New Music Friday edition. The two releases that genuinely match the watchlist's noise-rock / art-punk / post-punk lanes both drop today (Oct 9): Cameron Winter's Live at Carnegie Hall — the Geese frontman's rapturously reviewed live record, landed straight on the Official Charts NMF lineup and end-of-week lists — and BODEGA's All Inside Aquarium, their fourth album trading some post-punk angularity for guitar-driven rock. 1. Cameron Winter (of Geese) — Live at Carnegie Hall (out Friday, Oct 9)

The easy headline for the indie / art-rock end of your taste. Geese frontman Cameron Winter releases his full Carnegie Hall set — recorded at his historic sold-out show, mixed by Paul Thomas Anderson and pressed as a double live album with three unreleased studio songs — today via Partisan. It's been called an "immediate classic" (The Guardian) and "a star is born" (Paste), with Pitchfork's review landing this morning. For anyone who keeps Geese near the top of the noise-rock/art-punk lane, this is the week's most essential, on-lane purchase — a quiet, piano-heavy, theatrical counterpoint to Geese's own racket. Status: Out Oct 9. Sources: Official Charts NMF (Oct 9); Pitchfork review (Oct 9); The Guardian (Oct 2); Paste (Oct 8); Rolling Stone (Aug 19).

2. BODEGA — All Inside Aquarium (out Friday, Oct 9)

The post-punk / art-punk lane gets its release-of-the-week. New York's BODEGA drop their fourth album, All Inside Aquarium, today on Chrysalis. Reviews are already out (Glide, Oct 8; Tinnitist "Album of the Week") and they note the band cooling some of their trademark talky angularity in favor of clearer guitar-driven rock while keeping the sharp, satirical songwriting intact. The band also previewed it with an unexpected slow-groove Bob Dylan cover of "Slow Train" (That Eric Alper, Oct 8). It sits naturally beside the artier, wordier bands in your post-punk rotation. Status: Out Oct 9. Sources: Glide Magazine (Oct 8); That Eric Alper (Oct 8); Tinnitist (Oct 9); NME / DIY / Line of Best Fit (album announcement, Jun 22).
