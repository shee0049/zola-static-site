1. Tony Iommi — From the Dark (out Oct 23, BMG)

The genuinely big one for your classic / ancestry lane — Black Sabbath's guitarist finally returns. Tony Iommi announced From the Dark, billed as his first solo album in 21 years (he last stepped out solo on 2005's Fused), out October 23 via BMG. Previewed as a heavy record, it was announced late July (Rock Sound, Pitchfork, Stereogum, Ultimate Classic Rock all covered the reveal) and features Becky Baldwin on bass; an Ultimate-Guitar piece (Sept 11) reported Iommi deliberately banned guide tracks for her sessions, asking "What would Geezer Butler do?" to keep the bass playing loose and live. For anyone whose playlist reaches back to early Black Sabbath, this is the essential October release — the riff architect who essentially invented heavy metal's sound, with something new after two decades. Status: Out Oct 23. Sources: Pitchfork, Stereogum, Rock Sound, Ultimate Classic Rock (Jul–Sept 2026), Loudwire release calendar (2026-10-23), MusicBrainz (2026-10-23).

2. Hippotraktor — Annihilist (out this Friday, Oct 2, Pelagic)

The freshest, nearest item on the calendar and squarely your post-metal / sludge lane. Hippotraktor, the Belgian instrumental-leaning heavy outfit on Pelagic Records (the same label as The Ocean), release Annihilist on October 2. It lands as a new album (MusicBrainz distinguishes it from 2021's earlier single of the same name) with a 2026-10-02 date, and Pelagic's roster bias toward riff-heavy, atmospherically-driven metal makes it a strong watchful new-player pick in the Kowloon Walled City–to–post-rock part of the list. Status: Out Friday, Oct 2. Sources: Loudwire 2026 release calendar, MusicBrainz (2026-10-02).

3. Stoned Jesus — Songs to Moon (out Oct 16, Season of Mist)

For the sludge / stoner / doom wing, Kyiv's Stoned Jesus follow last year's Songs to Sun with Songs to Moon, out October 16 on Season of Mist — confirmed by both the Loudwire calendar and MusicBrainz (2026-10-16). The band is one of the most reliable riff-purveyors in the genre, and this pairs neatly thematically with 2025's Songs to Sun as a loose companion piece. If your Seven Thunders Roar rotates regularly, this is an easy October pre-order. Status: Out Oct 16. Sources: Loudwire 2026 release calendar, MusicBrainz (2026-10-16).
