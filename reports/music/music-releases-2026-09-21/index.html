Executive Summary

A week heavy on hardcore and post-punk for the watchlist. Soul Glo — the Philadelphia noise-hardcore trio already on your radar next to Chat Pile and Knocked Loose — announced a new album, NA I'M GON GRAB ALLAT, with heavyweight guests (Tunde Adebimpe, MIKE, BbyMutha). Echo & the Bunnymen released Apples for Isaac (Sept 18), a late-career post-punk return worth a spin for anyone who grew up on Ocean Rain. On the sludge/doom side of your taste, both Sleep's Hempispheres and Down's Volume V (lead single "Blood Oath") are out. Notable week-of news: the death of New Found Glory guitarist and producer Chad Gilbert at 45 rippled through the punk world.

1. Soul Glo — NA I'M GON GRAB ALLAT (announced)

The band that made your 2022 Diaspora Problems favorite is back. Soul Glo announced a new album, NA I'M GON GRAB ALLAT, and previewed it with the single "February Theory" (Sept 9). The guest list is the story: Tunde Adebimpe (TV on the Radio), MIKE, and BbyMutha all appear. If your taste leans toward literate, confrontational hardcore with a hard noise edge, this is the most-wanted item on today's list — and a direct cousin to the Chat Pile/Home Front lane in your collection.

Status: Announced — single out now, album TBD. Source: Glide Magazine (Sep 2026).

2. Echo & the Bunnymen — Apples for Isaac (out Sept 18)

Liverpool's post-punk legends returned with Apples for Isaac this week (Sept 18). It's their first proper studio album in years and, on early listen, keeps the moody, cinematic baritone-and-jangle sound intact. For the post-punk side of your taste — Viagra Boys, Home Front, Squid — this is the week's most conventional-but-reliable reference point. Status: Out now. Source: Rock Cellar Magazine (preview, out Sept 18).

3. Down — Volume V (out now; lead single "Blood Oath")

Southern-metal sludge crew Down dropped the lead single "Blood Oath" and confirmed Volume V as their next album. For the Black Sabbath / sludge end of your taste, Down remains a cornerstone — expect the thick, doomy riffs that signed NOLA and Down II. Status: Single out; album Volume V expected soon. Sources: Chaoszine, Consequence of Sound (Aug 2026).

4. Sleep — Hempispheres (out Sept 3)

The doom legends' first record without original guitarist Matt Pike landed Hempispheres earlier this month. Reviews (Glide, Revolver) called it a genuine new chapter rather than a farewell — the same cosmic-void space-doom sound, retooled. Essential for the Dopesmoker faithful on your watchlist. Status: Out now.
