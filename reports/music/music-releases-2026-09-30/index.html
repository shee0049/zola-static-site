Executive Summary

This Wednesday edition points at the Oct 2 New Music Friday. The headliners for that Friday were already covered in the Sept 28/29 reports (Tony Iommi's From the Dark on Oct 23; Hippotraktor's Annihilist, plus Swans, The Fall, Deerhoof, Fat Dog and Johnny Marr all due Oct 2), so today surfaces the deeper, watchlist-relevant cuts from the fresh Oct 2 release calendars, plus two brand-new Punknews announcements (Sept 29–30). The most on-taste arrivals: L.A. screamo/shoegaze-hardcore band Agriculture deliver the live The Spiritual Sound, Live in Europe 2026 (Oct 2 digital); Quebec City death/thrash unit Dissimulator land Artifice Dissolves (Oct 2, 20 Buck Spin — a Canadian pick); Italian stoner-psych outfit The Machine drop ANother Sun (Oct 2, Blues Funeral Recordings); and hardcore Bombstrap debut Big Enemy (Oct 2, Tartarus). Looking further out, London electro-punks Body Horror announced their debut EP At The Nepo for Oct 30. All are new to your radar — none appeared in earlier reports.

1. Agriculture — The Spiritual Sound, Live in Europe 2026 (out Oct 2 digital / Nov 27 physical)

The most on-playlist item of the new lot. LA's Agriculture — who make a gloriously noisy blend of blackgaze, screamo and shoegaze that sits squarely in the hardcore-adjacent wing of your playlist — have turned the songs from their acclaimed 2025 album The Spiritual Sound into a live record. The Spiritual Sound, Live in Europe 2026 was captured mostly during Roadburn 2026, with other dates from their European run, and features live versions of every track from the studio LP; the song "Bodhidharma" (live in Cologne) previews it. It arrives digitally on October 2, with a physical release on November 27, just before the band's US tour starting late October. If you've been following the Flenser-adjacent side of the list, this is the freshest thing of the week. Status: Digital out Fri, Oct 2; physical Nov 27. Sources: Punknews.org (Sept 29 2026), MusicBrainz (2026-10-02).

2. Dissimulator — Artifice Dissolves (out Oct 2, 20 Buck Spin)

The Canadian-heavy pick, and one of the week's most interesting technical records. Quebec City death/thrash trio Dissimulator return with Artifice Dissolves, their follow-up to 2024's well-regarded Ursurper (and their 20 Buck Spin releases more broadly). Previewed over a series of singles through 2026, the record leans into the band's signature precision — thrashy riffing, punishing drums and trebly, technical-leaning death metal — with a progressive, almost jittery energy. 20 Buck Spin has been a reliably excellent home for the weirder end of modern extreme metal, which makes this an easy pick for anyone whose heavier tastes drift toward the Kowloon Walled City–to–post-metal part of the list. It's also a neat Canadian-angle addition to the report. Status: Out Fri, Oct 2. Sources: The Razor's Edge Release Round Up (Sept 26 2026), MusicBrainz (2026-10-02).

3. The Machine — ANother Sun (out Oct 2, Blues Funeral Recordings)

For the stoner / psychedelic / sludge lane, Italian rockers The Machine (long-running purveyors of heavy, melodic stoner-psych) return with ANother Sun on Blues Funeral Recordings — the same label that's been putting out quality instrumental-heavy rock from this exact scene. MusicBrainz confirms the Oct 2 date and lists it as a full album. The Machine's sound — thick fuzz, fuzzed-out leads and hypnotic space-rock stretches — sits nicely next to the Windhand-to-stoner end of your list, and it's a safe bet for fans of riff-forward instrumental-leaning rock. Status: Out Fri, Oct 2. Sources: The Razor's Edge Release Round Up (Sept 26 2026), MusicBrainz (2026-10-02).

4. Bombstrap — Big Enemy (out Oct 2, Tartarus Records)

A straight hardcore entry for that lane. Bombstrap's Big Enemy arrives Oct 2 via Tartarus Records — listed in this week's heavy release roundup. Hardcore outfit best known for short, belligerent bursts of fast punk-metal, Big Enemy is a debut-album check-in for the band (MusicBrainz tags it a full-length). For the Knocked Loose / Drug Church / hardcore side of the playlist, it's a low-risk, high-context New Music Friday addition — exactly the kind of under-the-radar debut these roundups are useful for. Status: Out Fri, Oct 2. Sources: The Razor's Edge Release Round Up (Sept 26 2026), MusicBrainz (2026-10-02).

5. Body Horror — At The Nepo (debut EP, out Oct 30)

The fresh punk announcement of the week. London electro-punks Body Horror — who have been dropping singles since 2021 — announced their debut EP, At The Nepo, out October 30, and shared the new song "The Nepo a Go Go" to go with it. It's the raucous, dancefloor-nasty electro-punk lane (think the rowdier end of post-punk/art-punk), squarely a watch for the post-punk / punk side of the playlist. Status: Out Oct 30. Sources: Punknews.org (Sept 29 2026).
